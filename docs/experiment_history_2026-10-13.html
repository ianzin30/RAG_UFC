Why the study changed

Start from the retrieval failure, then choose the intervention

The research goal: place the complete, relevant, source-supported evidence for the question in the final context. Preserve indirect relations and competing document identities within a usable budget, using mechanisms that do not depend on the answer key.

Why LLM diagnostics appear here: an LLM can choose queries upstream of retrieval. If its input or decision fails, we cannot test the benefit of its searches. Fixing that readiness is necessary, but is not itself an improvement in retrieval.

What “complete” means here: all annotated documentary requirements appear in the selected context. It is not answer accuracy. Most retrieval rounds block final-answer generation.

Two scopes: collection searches the full index; restricted supplies the correct documents as a privileged diagnostic control. Do not interpret the restricted result as automatic document discovery.

General mechanisms do not require extra questions. Extra held-out questions can provide evidence of transfer, but they do not make a mechanism general by themselves.

What was held fixed

Earlier rounds are not one hyperparameter-controlled experiment

The current full-versus-minimal pair shares its model, sampling, input policy and limits. Across rounds, provider, model, window, output cap, timeout and prompt changed. Comparisons across those profiles are diagnostic, not clean estimates of one architecture change.

Current shared sampling: top-p 0.95, top-k 20, min-p 0, repeat penalty 1.0; seed 42. Both contracts explicitly enable thinking. The 4,096-token output cap covers thinking and the decision together.

Current shared retrieval: Gemma dense, BGE-M3 sparse, BGE-Reranker; 30 per channel; rerank the full accumulated union; whole-parent global selection; at most four total searches and four model decisions. Private thinking is not fed into the next observation.

The tested change is broader than shorter JSON. Minimal decisions remove mandatory needs, dependencies and support quotations. Their sufficiency declaration is unverified. More accepted decisions alone cannot establish better investigation.

Assessment before the next step

Keep the findings; limit the claims

Retain the candidate-union discovery

It recovered useful facts without new models, rules or searches. It is a general retrieval mechanism with a controlled benefit on this corpus.

Keep alternation conditional

Its strongest result depended on focused queries. Generic query generation often displaced evidence. Evaluate the query policy and selection together, while keeping their effects separable.

Do not promote the adaptive agent yet

The Phi-4 comparison was interrupted. Model/backend pilots were readiness diagnostics. The current simpler contract has an operational pilot, not completed documentary-quality evidence.

Freeze one profile before another intervention

Finish the existing paired study, inspect gains and regressions, then change one new factor at a time. Do not turn model switches or context-preservation replay into agent-reasoning gains.

Historical conclusions: full union was a candidate and then confirmed in the applied configuration; continuous grouping was revise; fixed-field searches/alternation were a candidate on the original questions; semantic planning was revise; this MMR configuration was discard; LLM evidence selection remained incomplete.
